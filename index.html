About The Project

This project focuses on addressing water scarcity and contamination by designing a low-cost, multi-stage water purifier using accessible materials like activated carbon, sand, and gravel. The objective is to convert non-potable "dirty" water into usable water for domestic purposes. Findings indicate a significant reduction in turbidity and chemical impurities, proving the system's viability for rural or emergency applications.

Global Water Crisis

As of 2026, the world is facing what the UN calls "Global Water Bankruptcy."

Scarcity

Over 4 billion people experience severe water scarcity for at least one month per year.

Safety Gap

Approximately 2.2 billion people still lack access to safely managed drinking water.

Depletion

50% of the world's large lakes have lost water volume since the 1990s, and 70% of major aquifers are in decline.

Economic Impact

The annual global cost of drought has reached approximately $307 billion.

Stages of Treatment

1

Preliminary Screening

Physical barriers like bar screens prevent sticks, plastics, and dead organic matter from entering, protecting mechanical pumps.

2

Aeration

Water is saturated with oxygen to "degas" it, releasing hydrogen sulphide and oxidizing dissolved iron and manganese.

3

Coagulation & Flocculation

Chemicals like Alum neutralize dirt particles. Gentle stirring encourages neutralized particles to collide and stick, forming "flocs."

4

Sedimentation

In a quiet basin, heavy flocs settle to the bottom as sludge via gravity, while clear effluent water moves to the next stage.

5

Multi-Media Filtration

Water passes through layers (Anthracite, Sand, Gravel) to trap particles, highly effective at removing "turbidity" (cloudiness).

6

Adsorption (Activated Carbon)

Granular Activated Carbon pulls out pesticides, industrial solvents, and chlorine taste like a "chemical magnet."

7

Disinfection

The final critical safety stage kills pathogens via Chlorination, UV Radiation, or Ozonation.

The Prototype

Design Features

  • The Vessel: Clear acrylic cylinder for visibility.
  • The Intake: Small 5V or 12V DC water pump.
  • Real-Time Monitoring: Integrated pH probe (Ideal pH = 7.0 ± 0.5).
  • Backwashing Feature: Reverse clean water flow to remove sludge.
  • Post-Filtration Disinfection: Final treatment with chlorine drops (Sodium Hypochlorite) to eliminate microscopic pathogens and ensure drinking safety.

Materials Used

  • Structural: Plastic Bottle, Collection Container, Coffee Filter/Cheesecloth.
  • Filtration Media: Activated Charcoal, Fine Sand, Coarse Sand, Gravel.
  • Testing: Contaminated Water Sample, pH Meter, TDS Meter.
  • Chemical Purifier: Unscented Chlorine Drops or Water Purification Tablets.

Water Quality Indicators

BOD

Biochemical Oxygen Demand. High BOD = High Pollution.

DO

Dissolved Oxygen. Healthy water > 5 mg/L.

Hardness

Calcium & Magnesium concentration.

Coliform

Test for E. coli indicating contamination.
